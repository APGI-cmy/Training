SUMMARY
The Scannex Operator Role. Summary. Competent screening combines technical knowledge, consistent procedure, careful interpretation, professional integrity and safety awareness. Each capability supports the others.

STATE-01
Technical understanding. Know how the system works, what it can detect and how to use its approved functions correctly. Good decisions start with correct operation.

Key takeaway: Correct operation is the starting point for a sound decision. Why this matters: Correct operation is the starting point for a sound decision. The full-body screening and operator monitor illustrate correct use of approved equipment. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photograph of the installed system.

STATE-02
Procedural discipline. Follow the approved process consistently. Consistency is a security control because it reduces gaps created by pressure, routine or personal judgment.

Key takeaway: Consistency closes gaps created by pressure and routine. Why this matters: Consistency closes gaps created by pressure and routine. Procedural discipline: consistency is a security control.

STATE-03
Interpretive skill. Review images carefully and distinguish a possible concealed object from normal anatomy, clothing or everyday items. Careful interpretation supports a defensible decision.

Key takeaway: Possible concealment must be distinguished from normal anatomy and everyday items. Why this matters: Possible concealment must be distinguished from normal anatomy and everyday items. The operator examines a human-body image. The learning task is to distinguish normal structures from a possible concealed object; this illustration is not an operational scan.

STATE-04
Professional integrity. Treat every person fairly, resist improper influence and protect dignity. Impartial conduct protects the person being screened and the integrity of the operation.

Key takeaway: Impartial conduct protects dignity and the integrity of the process. Why this matters: Impartial conduct protects dignity and the integrity of the process. The balance symbolises impartial judgement: treat people fairly and resist improper influence. It is a conceptual illustration.

STATE-05
Safety awareness. Respect radiation controls, reporting requirements and authorised operating boundaries. Safe operation remains inseparable from effective operation.

Key takeaway: Effective operation remains inseparable from safe operation. Why this matters: Effective operation remains inseparable from safe operation.
