SUMMARY
Where X-rays Fit. Summary. X-rays are produced only when needed and are controlled because their high energy can affect atoms. That same property makes careful image-based screening possible.

STATE-01
One electromagnetic family. Radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays are all forms of electromagnetic energy.

Key takeaway: X-rays belong to the electromagnetic family. Why this matters: X-rays belong to the electromagnetic family. Follow the labelled electromagnetic families from radio waves to gamma rays. The highlighted region identifies X-rays.

STATE-02
Short wavelength, high energy. X-rays have a much shorter wavelength and higher energy than visible light. This allows them to pass through materials that visible light cannot.

Key takeaway: Compared with visible light, X-rays have a shorter wavelength and higher energy. Why this matters: Compared with visible light, X-rays have a shorter wavelength and higher energy. The waves compare wavelength and energy. Shorter wavelength corresponds to higher photon energy.

STATE-03
Why X-rays are ionising. X-rays can remove an electron from an atom. This process is called ionisation and is why exposure must be controlled. Image contrast results from differences in how materials attenuate X-rays.

Key takeaway: Ionisation means removing an electron from an atom. Why this matters: Ionisation means removing an electron from an atom. The incoming photon ejects an electron and leaves an ionised atom. Exposure controls address this effect.

STATE-04
Produced only when needed. Scannex generates X-rays on demand inside a sealed tube. They do not exist before the scan and do not remain after the system stops.

This statement refers to X-rays produced by the tube. It does not mean that background radiation elsewhere disappears.

Key takeaway: The tube produces X-rays during operation; they are not stored in the person. Why this matters: The tube produces X-rays during operation; they are not stored in the person. Compare the tube switched off, operating and switched off again. X-ray generation ends when production stops.
