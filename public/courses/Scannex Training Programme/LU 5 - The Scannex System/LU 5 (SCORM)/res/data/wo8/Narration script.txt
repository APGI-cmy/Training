INTRO
Shift Checklist. Learning outcome: Explain Scannex system safeguards and segregated roles, apply the supplied scan workflow, respond through authorised support, and maintain quality checks and records. This activity explains shift checklist. Select each labelled control, read the complete narrative, enlarge the illustration and review the summary before its knowledge check. Follow the current approved site procedure; these illustrations are conceptual teaching examples. Narration is optional.

SUMMARY
Shift Checklist. Summary. Resolve a failed readiness check before the first scan. Explain each step and use the approved positioning procedure. Protect role separation throughout the encounter. Record the authorised decision and any repeat accurately.

STATE-01
Before the first scan. Before the first person enters, confirm that no fault is displayed, the cubicle is clear and the position markers are visible. If any check fails, stop and report it before scanning begins.

Key takeaway: Resolve a failed readiness check before the first scan. Why this matters: Resolve a failed readiness check before the first scan. The full-body chamber and specialist’s record illustrate checking current inspection and release status before the first person enters.

STATE-02
For every person. For every person, confirm consent, explain the next step and assist with the marked standing position. Ask them to place keys, cards and similar items between their feet before the scan starts.

The item-placement instruction comes from the supplied course protocol. Follow the current manufacturer and approved site instructions for the installed system; do not invent a different position.

Key takeaway: Explain each step and use the approved positioning procedure. Why this matters: Explain each step and use the approved positioning procedure. The outline identifies the positioning panel in the routine scan sequence. Confirm consent and use the current approved item-placement instructions.

STATE-03
Protect the process. Protect role separation: the Scan Operator manages the person and procedure, while the Viewer interprets the image. Handle any possible indication through the defined escalation process, never through personal judgement.

Key takeaway: Protect role separation throughout the encounter. Why this matters: Protect role separation throughout the encounter. The independent Viewer’s full-body display illustrates keeping interpretation separate from person management.

STATE-04
Complete the record. Use fresh image angles only for an authorised re-scan, then complete the required logbook and incident records accurately. Clear records show that each decision followed the approved process.

Key takeaway: Record the authorised decision and any repeat accurately. Why this matters: Record the authorised decision and any repeat accurately. The record book illustrates completing the machine logbook and incident record after the authorised decision.
