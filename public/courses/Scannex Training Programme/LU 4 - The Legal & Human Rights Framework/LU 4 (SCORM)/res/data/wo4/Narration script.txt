INTRO
The Lawful Scan Decision. Learning outcome: Apply the supplied Scannex legal and human-rights protocol: lawful authority, informed consent, privacy, dignity, neutral selection, proportionate response and authorised escalation. This activity explains the lawful scan decision. Select each labelled control, read the complete narrative, enlarge the highlighted illustration and review the summary before the knowledge check. Apply the supplied protocol through the current approved local procedure.

SUMMARY
The Lawful Scan Decision. Summary. Check the applicable procedure before beginning. Consent must be informed and voluntary. Selection must be fair, consistent and non-discriminatory. A lawful scan is also a respectful scan. Pausing protects everyone when certainty is missing.

STATE-01
Authority. Confirm that the local law and approved Scannex procedure allow a scan in this setting. An operational concern alone is not a legal basis.

Key takeaway: Check the applicable procedure before beginning. Why this matters: Check the applicable procedure before beginning. Stage 1, left: check the approved authority and local procedure before beginning.

STATE-02
Consent. Explain what the scan involves, why it is being used, the approved dose information and radiation-protection arrangements and the record process. Obtain informed consent before the scan proceeds.

Key takeaway: Consent must be informed and voluntary. Why this matters: Consent must be informed and voluntary. Stage 2: the operator explains the proposal and checks informed, voluntary consent.

STATE-03
Neutrality. Use the approved random access-control selection process exactly as it is generated. Do not change it to target or spare a person.

Key takeaway: Selection must be fair, consistent and non-discriminatory. Why this matters: Selection must be fair, consistent and non-discriminatory. Stage 3: the pictured encounter belongs to the approved selection process; it does not authorise personal selection or a new physical-search procedure.

STATE-04
Respect. Protect privacy and dignity throughout the encounter. Use calm language and the least intrusive effective method.

Key takeaway: A lawful scan is also a respectful scan. Why this matters: A lawful scan is also a respectful scan. Stage 4: calm communication and the screening setting illustrate protecting the person’s privacy and dignity.

STATE-05
Uncertainty. If the lawful basis, consent or next step is unclear, stop. Do not scan and do not improvise. Escalate to the supervisor or authorised decision-maker.

Key takeaway: Pausing protects everyone when certainty is missing. Why this matters: Pausing protects everyone when certainty is missing. Stage 5, right: the raised stop gesture and supervisor discussion illustrate pausing when the lawful next step is uncertain.
