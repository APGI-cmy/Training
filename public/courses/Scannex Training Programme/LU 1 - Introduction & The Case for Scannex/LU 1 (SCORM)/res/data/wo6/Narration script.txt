STATE-02
Foundation glossary. Screening: An approved process used to examine people or items for a security purpose.

Concealment: Keeping an item hidden from ordinary observation.

Reviewable image: A recorded image that supports examination and a decision based on evidence.

Procedural discipline: Following the approved process consistently.

Professional integrity: Fair and impartial conduct that resists improper influence.

Loss prevention: Measures that reduce the opportunity for loss and protect lawful value.

Key takeaway: Use these terms to explain the operator’s responsibilities accurately.
