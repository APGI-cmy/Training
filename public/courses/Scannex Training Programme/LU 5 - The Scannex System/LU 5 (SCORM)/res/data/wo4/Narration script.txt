INTRO
Roles and Cubicle Safeguards. Learning outcome: Explain Scannex system safeguards and segregated roles, apply the supplied scan workflow, respond through authorised support, and maintain quality checks and records. This activity explains roles and cubicle safeguards. Select each labelled control, read the complete narrative, enlarge the illustration and review the summary before its knowledge check. Follow the current approved site procedure; these illustrations are conceptual teaching examples. Narration is optional.

SUMMARY
Roles and Cubicle Safeguards. Summary. The Scan Operator manages the person and procedure, not the image decision. The independent Viewer examines the image through the separated role. A clear cubicle and correct positioning support a consistent scan. Surveillance adds oversight without taking over the other roles.

STATE-01
Scan Operator. The Scan Operator explains the procedure, confirms consent, assists with correct positioning and starts the approved scan. The operator must not view or interpret the image; that task belongs to the independent Viewer.

Key takeaway: The Scan Operator manages the person and procedure, not the image decision. Why this matters: The Scan Operator manages the person and procedure, not the image decision. The operator guides a person at the full-body chamber. This illustrates communication and positioning, separate from image interpretation.

STATE-02
Viewer. The Viewer independently examines scan images but does not meet the person being scanned. This separation protects impartial decision-making and prevents one person from controlling both scanning and image interpretation.

The supplied separation protocol also requires no direct contact between the Viewer and the Scan Operator. Use the authorised communication and escalation route; do not bypass role separation.

Key takeaway: The independent Viewer examines the image through the separated role. Why this matters: The independent Viewer examines the image through the separated role. The operator seated at a display of a full-body image represents the independent Viewer. Follow the supplied separation protocol.

STATE-03
Cubicle. The cubicle is a sealed, clutter-free standing area with no accessible moving parts and a fixed scan duration. Keep it clear, use the marked standing position and report damage, obstruction or abnormal conditions.

Key takeaway: A clear cubicle and correct positioning support a consistent scan. Why this matters: A clear cubicle and correct positioning support a consistent scan. The outline identifies the positioning panel, second from the left. The standing area and markers illustrate the controlled cubicle, not a conveyor scanner.

STATE-04
Surveillance. A separate operator observes cameras around the cubicle and maintains wider situational awareness. This extra oversight supports safety and accountability while the Scan Operator focuses on the person and procedure.

Key takeaway: Surveillance adds oversight without taking over the other roles. Why this matters: Surveillance adds oversight without taking over the other roles. The camera displays represent separate surveillance of the screening setting. Surveillance does not replace the independent Viewer’s image decision.
