# App Description Addendum — W4.2 Learner Management Experience

**Status:** Product-owner authorised 2026-08-11; safe UI and read-only data scope only.

The administration area shall provide an APGI-branded learner-management experience inspired by the operational clarity of the supplied Thinkific references, without copying Thinkific branding or granting unapproved write authority.

The experience includes a searchable learner directory, learner identity and enrolment summaries, invitation drafting with transparent no-send posture, staged CSV bulk-import validation, and a full-page administrator course preview. Learner account creation, invitation creation, enrolment change, email sending and import execution remain deliberately disabled until the test learner and lifecycle are explicitly agreed.

The design must remain responsive, keyboard-accessible, role-gated and data-minimising. Administrator preview must never write learner progress or enrolment state.
