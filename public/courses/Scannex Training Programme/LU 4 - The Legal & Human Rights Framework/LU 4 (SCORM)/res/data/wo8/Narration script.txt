SUMMARY
Learning Unit 4: Recap. Summary. Every scan requires the approved legal and safety framework. Use informed consent under the supplied protocol, protect privacy and dignity, apply neutral selection consistently and respond proportionately. Record facts, protect evidence, respect continuing rights and escalate uncertainty.

STATE-01
Unit recap. The Legal and Human-Rights Framework: This prevents an operator from treating a scan as routine simply because the equipment is available. Privacy protects the person and preserves the integrity of the operation. Respect and consistency make the process humane and defensible. A lawful response protects security without unnecessarily limiting a person’s rights.

The Lawful Scan Decision: Check the applicable procedure before beginning. Consent must be informed and voluntary. Selection must be fair, consistent and non-discriminatory. A lawful scan is also a respectful scan. Pausing protects everyone when certainty is missing.

A Positive Indication: Lawful Response: Do not accuse, speculate or make a judgement. Reliable records protect the person and the investigation. The procedure tells you what is permitted. Bring in the correct authority promptly. Professional conduct is required at every stage.

Rights During Detention: Use calm, factual communication and maintain humane conditions. An indication does not cancel fair treatment. Do not obstruct authorised communication. These needs are rights, not optional comforts. Escalate promptly when specialised support or protection is needed.

Every Scan. Every Time: Sensitive information must never become casual information. Security does not remove a person’s right to respectful treatment. Consistency prevents discrimination and builds trust. The response must never go further than the risk justifies.

Key takeaway: Apply all safeguards together in every encounter. Why this matters: Apply all safeguards together in every encounter. The four corners show privacy, dignity, consistency and proportionality around the full-body screening setting.

STATE-02
Before and during the scan. Consent: Explain the approved purpose, process, dose information and record handling before seeking informed voluntary agreement.

Privacy and lawful retention: Use authorised access, secure storage and the approved retention process.

Dignity and consistency: Communicate calmly and apply the same neutral selection rules.

Proportionality: Choose the least intrusive effective response justified by the lawful purpose.

When uncertain: Pause and escalate through the approved procedure. The course does not establish the legal authority for a specific site.

Key takeaway: Pause whenever the lawful authority or consent is unclear. Why this matters: Pause whenever the lawful authority or consent is unclear. Authority, privacy, neutral selection and escalation are shown as complementary safeguards.

STATE-03
Indications and lawful follow-up. A positive indication requires factual follow-up and does not establish guilt. Secure the image and record, apply the approved local procedure and summon authorised support. Treat the person humanely throughout.

A detained person retains rights, including humane conditions, information and lawful contact. Formal arrest decisions follow applicable local authority. Preserve accurate records for independent legal review.

The knowledge checks are learning exercises, not the supervised practical assessment. Revisit the glossary or matching activity when uncertain.

Key takeaway: An indication is a reason for authorised review, not an accusation. Why this matters: An indication is a reason for authorised review, not an accusation. The five stages connect a possible indication, secure records, approved procedure, authorised support and humane treatment.
