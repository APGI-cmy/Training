INTRO
Scan workflow. Learning outcome: Explain Scannex system safeguards and segregated roles, apply the supplied scan workflow, respond through authorised support, and maintain quality checks and records. This activity explains scan workflow. Select each labelled control, read the complete narrative, enlarge the illustration and review the summary before its knowledge check. Follow the current approved site procedure; these illustrations are conceptual teaching examples. Narration is optional.

SUMMARY
Scan workflow. Summary. Confirm consent respectfully before scanning. Use the approved marked positions and personal-item procedure. Keep the person still and use the approved fixed settings. Wait for the approved completion signal before directing the person to leave. A routine holding period is not an accusation.

STATE-01
Consent first. Under the supplied course protocol, consent is a condition of entry, but it must still be confirmed respectfully with each person before scanning begins. Explain what will happen and do not force or rush the person. Apply the current authorised consent and referral procedure.

Key takeaway: Confirm consent respectfully before scanning. Why this matters: Confirm consent respectfully before scanning. The outline identifies panel 1: the operator explains the scan to the person.

STATE-02
Position correctly. Guide the person to the marked foot positions and ask them to follow the head-turn marker. Place keys, cards and similar items between the feet so they do not create misleading image features.

The item-placement instruction comes from the supplied course protocol. Follow the current manufacturer and approved site instructions for the installed system; do not invent a different position.

Key takeaway: Use the approved marked positions and personal-item procedure. Why this matters: Use the approved marked positions and personal-item procedure. The outline identifies panel 2: the person uses the marked position in the full-body chamber.

STATE-03
Scan safely. Ask the person to remain still while the automated system carries out the controlled scan. Follow the approved procedure and do not alter equipment settings.

Key takeaway: Keep the person still and use the approved fixed settings. Why this matters: Keep the person still and use the approved fixed settings. The outline covers panels 3 and 4: the authorised start is followed by remaining still during the controlled scan.

STATE-04
Exit on red light. The red exit light confirms that the scan is complete and the person may leave the cubicle. Do not direct someone to leave early; the light is the approved completion signal.

This is the completion indicator described in the supplied course protocol, not a fault alarm. Follow the actual installed system’s approved signal instructions; a red fault warning requires stopping and reporting the fault.

Key takeaway: Wait for the approved completion signal before directing the person to leave. Why this matters: Wait for the approved completion signal before directing the person to leave. The outline identifies panel 5: the supplied course depicts the red completion signal. Check its exact meaning on the actual installed system.

STATE-05
Holding area. The holding area is a short, routine wait while the Viewer assesses the image. Explain this calmly as part of the normal process; do not suggest that a person has done anything wrong.

Key takeaway: A routine holding period is not an accusation. Why this matters: A routine holding period is not an accusation. The outline identifies panel 6: exit and the routine wait for independent review complete the illustrated sequence.
