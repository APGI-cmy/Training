SUMMARY
Safe Operating Discipline. Summary. Professional operation means using only authorised equipment and settings, applying exclusions, stopping on faults and keeping accurate records.

STATE-01
Confirm authorisation and QA. Operate only when you are trained, authorised and current, and the equipment is within its QA date.

Key takeaway: Confirm training, authorisation and current QA before use. Why this matters: Confirm training, authorisation and current QA before use. Authorised, QA current and Ready are linked. These checks precede operation. 

STATE-02
Apply ALARA. Use the approved settings and controls. Never bypass filtering, collimation, interlocks or exposure controls to save time.

Key takeaway: Use approved settings and respect every safety control. Why this matters: Use approved settings and respect every safety control. Protected settings lead to the low-dose indicator. The image represents approved controls, not permission to adjust the equipment. ALARA means keeping exposure as low as reasonably achievable while still completing the authorised task.

STATE-03
Apply exclusions discreetly. Check exclusions every time. Arrange the approved alternative procedure privately and respectfully when needed.

Key takeaway: Apply exclusions discreetly on every occasion. Why this matters: Apply exclusions discreetly on every occasion. Follow the pictured route from eligibility checking to private discussion and an alternative when required. Consistency prevents both unnecessary exposure and unfair treatment.

STATE-04
Stop and report faults. Do not operate faulty equipment or equipment with overdue checks. Stop use and report the condition to the RPO.

For a safety concern: stop the scan or do not start; keep the person safe and calm; record the concern or abnormal condition; report to the supervisor or RPO; do not use the equipment again until cleared. Follow the approved emergency procedure immediately where urgent action is needed.

Key takeaway: Stop use, protect the person and report a safety concern. Why this matters: Stop use, protect the person and report a safety concern. Follow the fault symbol to Stop use and Report to RPO. The diagram represents the approved reporting route. 

STATE-05
Keep records accurate. Log each exposure correctly and support the required dose-record checks.

Key takeaway: Accurate records make exposure and safety accountable. Why this matters: Accurate records make exposure and safety accountable. Accurate recording supports accountable safety. Complete the actual approved records; this is a conceptual illustration. Accurate records make it possible to review trends, confirm compliance and respond to any unexpected issue.

STATE-06
Explain safety calmly. Explain that the scan is measured, low and controlled through approved safeguards. Explain the procedure calmly and factually. Use verified dose information and the approved protection programme; refer detailed dose questions to the RPO. Do not make an unsupported comparison with a chest X-ray or a flight.

Key takeaway: Explain the controls calmly without an unsupported dose comparison. Why this matters: Explain the controls calmly without an unsupported dose comparison. An operator speaks calmly beside the full-body chamber. The scene illustrates respectful communication rather than a numerical dose claim.
