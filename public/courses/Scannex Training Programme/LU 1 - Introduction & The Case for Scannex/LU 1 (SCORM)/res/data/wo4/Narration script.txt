INTRO
Diamond Value and Loss Prevention. Learning outcome: Connect the value of a diamond to the operator’s role in protecting the recovery process. A small diamond represents a long and costly recovery process. Follow the stages to see why careful screening protects more than a single asset. Enlarge the images to examine their detail. Narration is optional. Complete the corresponding knowledge check after the activity summary.

SUMMARY
Diamond Value and Loss Prevention. Summary. Diamond value is concentrated, while recovery requires sustained work and investment. Consistent screening protects the lawful chain, honest employees and the wider operation.

STATE-01
Extensive recovery effort. Recovering diamonds requires substantial investment in people, equipment, energy and processing. Every recovered stone reflects work completed across the operation.

Key takeaway: A recovered stone represents work and investment across the operation. Why this matters: A recovered stone represents work and investment across the operation. The mine and processing plant show the equipment and work involved before a stone is recovered.

STATE-02
Exceptional value in a small object. A diamond can be physically tiny yet financially significant. Its small size can make the loss easy to underestimate, even though the consequence can be substantial.

Key takeaway: A tiny object may carry substantial financial value. Why this matters: A tiny object may carry substantial financial value. The small stone and everyday objects illustrate the difference between physical size and financial significance.

STATE-03
Theft breaks the chain. When a recovered diamond leaves the lawful process, the operation cannot recover its value through legitimate sale. The loss affects the entire chain that created the stone’s value.

Key takeaway: Theft interrupts the lawful recovery of value. Why this matters: Theft interrupts the lawful recovery of value. The linked stages represent a lawful recovery and security chain. If a stone leaves that chain, its legitimate sale value is lost to the operation.

STATE-04
Wider consequences. Stolen stones can support illicit trading and weaken the revenue that sustains operations and employment. Loss prevention protects the organisation and the people who depend on it.

Key takeaway: Loss prevention protects revenue, operations and people. Why this matters: Loss prevention protects revenue, operations and people. The connected workforce, facilities and community illustrate the wider people and operations supported by lawful revenue.

STATE-05
The operator protects shared value. Consistent screening reduces opportunity, supports honest employees and strengthens confidence in the process. The operator contributes directly to loss prevention.

Key takeaway: Consistent screening contributes directly to protecting shared value. Why this matters: Consistent screening contributes directly to protecting shared value. The diamond and balance connect valuable material with fair, consistent decisions that protect shared value.
