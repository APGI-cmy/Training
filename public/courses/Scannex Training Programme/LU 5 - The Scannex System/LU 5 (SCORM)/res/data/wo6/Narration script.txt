INTRO
Positive Indication and Re-scan. Learning outcome: Explain Scannex system safeguards and segregated roles, apply the supplied scan workflow, respond through authorised support, and maintain quality checks and records. This activity explains positive indication and re-scan. Select each labelled control, read the complete narrative, enlarge the illustration and review the summary before its knowledge check. Follow the current approved site procedure; these illustrations are conceptual teaching examples. Narration is optional.

SUMMARY
Positive Indication and Re-scan. Summary. An indication requires authorised review; it does not establish guilt. Use the designated support person and senior person on shift. Use a changed body position only for a justified, authorised repeat. Follow the specific facility procedure and record facts.

STATE-01
Defined response. A possible indication is an image finding that requires the approved response; it is not proof of guilt and does not make the Scan Operator the decision-maker. Stay calm, preserve dignity and follow the facility procedure.

Key takeaway: An indication requires authorised review; it does not establish guilt. Why this matters: An indication requires authorised review; it does not establish guilt. The marked feature on the full-body display represents a possible indication. It is a training illustration, not proof of concealed contraband.

STATE-02
Authorised support. Summon the designated support person and inform the senior person on shift so the Viewer is properly assisted. Do not manage an indication alone or call an unauthorised person into the process.

Key takeaway: Use the designated support person and senior person on shift. Why this matters: Use the designated support person and senior person on shift. The supervisor discussion represents designated support and the senior person on shift reviewing the authorised next step.

STATE-03
Re-scan. A justified re-scan uses a changed body position to provide a fresh image angle. Repeat only as authorised, explain the reason respectfully and stop when the Viewer and supervisor are satisfied.

A repeat is not automatic and must not be performed simply to obtain certainty. Follow the applicable authorisation, radiation-protection restrictions and approved site response.

Key takeaway: Use a changed body position only for a justified, authorised repeat. Why this matters: Use a changed body position only for a justified, authorised repeat. The changed posture in the full-body chamber represents a fresh image angle. It does not prescribe an unauthorised posture or permit unlimited repeat scans.

STATE-04
Other contraband. If the image indicates other contraband, follow the specific facility procedure for warnings, records and disciplinary escalation. Keep communication factual and respectful; do not invent a response.

Key takeaway: Follow the specific facility procedure and record facts. Why this matters: Follow the specific facility procedure and record facts. The calm discussion illustrates factual, respectful communication through the facility’s procedure. It does not show an independent finding of guilt.
