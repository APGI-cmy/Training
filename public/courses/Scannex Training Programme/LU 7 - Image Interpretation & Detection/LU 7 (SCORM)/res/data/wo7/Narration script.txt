INTRO
Learning Unit 7: Summary. Learning outcome: Use a structured method to recognise normal patterns, examine anomalies and escalate unresolved doubt. Review the learning unit before completing the final checks. Revisit any activity where you still have uncertainty. Use the optional Viewer practice to become familiar with the available controls; practice does not replace the knowledge checks or an authorised assessment.

SUMMARY
Key takeaway: Use the glossary accurately, understand low contrast and masking, trace plausible concealment routes, recognise normal anatomy, and apply the full image-review cycle. The Viewer examines and interprets; unresolved doubt follows the authorised response chain.

STATE-01
Interpret with a consistent method. Optimise the display and scan systematically. Account for normal anatomy and isolate anything unexplained. Test an anomaly with several viewing modes and combine the discriminators. Compare the same region with an earlier image when one is available. Resolve remaining doubt through senior staff and the authorised response chain; any repeat exposure or search must follow approved procedures and preserve dignity.

Key takeaway: Explain normal anatomy, investigate anomalies and escalate unresolved doubt. Texture helps distinguish normal bone from a uniformly dense isolated feature.
