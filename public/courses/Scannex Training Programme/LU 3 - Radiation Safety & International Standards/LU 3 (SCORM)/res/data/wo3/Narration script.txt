SUMMARY
Protection Exclusions and Alternatives. Summary. Protection exclusions are applied consistently, privately and without improvisation. Where a person cannot be scanned, the approved alternative procedure is used.

STATE-01
Eligible to proceed?. Only proceed when the person is eligible and the approved process has been followed.

Key takeaway: Check eligibility before exposure. Why this matters: Check eligibility before exposure. The green eligibility marker leads to the full-body screening chamber. Confirm eligibility before proceeding. Eligibility is checked before exposure, because a calm early decision prevents an unnecessary scan.

STATE-02
Pregnant or may be pregnant?. Do not use X-ray screening for a pregnant person or someone who may be pregnant. Handle the discussion privately and respectfully.

Key takeaway: Apply the pregnancy exclusion privately and use the approved alternative. Why this matters: Apply the pregnancy exclusion privately and use the approved alternative. The private discussion leads to an alternative route. Use this route when the pregnancy exclusion applies; the picture does not diagnose pregnancy. Do not debate the issue in public; use the approved referral or alternative pathway.

STATE-03
Medically restricted?. Do not scan anyone who is medically restricted from exposure to ionising radiation. Follow the approved alternative procedure.

Key takeaway: Respect a medical restriction and follow the approved alternative. Why this matters: Respect a medical restriction and follow the approved alternative. The medical-restriction symbol leads to an alternative procedure. Follow the approved site pathway. When uncertain, stop and ask the designated supervisor or safety contact rather than making an assumption.

STATE-04
Under 16?. Do not use X-ray screening for anyone under the age of 16. Children and adolescents are more radiosensitive.

Key takeaway: The supplied course protocol excludes anyone under 16 from X-ray screening. Why this matters: The supplied course protocol excludes anyone under 16 from X-ray screening. The labelled Under 16 decision leads to No X-ray scan and a private alternative route. This depicts the supplied course protocol. Confirm the approved alternative procedure promptly so that security and dignity are both maintained.
