INTRO
Every Scan. Every Time. Learning outcome: Apply the supplied Scannex legal and human-rights protocol: lawful authority, informed consent, privacy, dignity, neutral selection, proportionate response and authorised escalation. This activity explains every scan. every time. Select each labelled control, read the complete narrative, enlarge the highlighted illustration and review the summary before the knowledge check. Apply the supplied protocol through the current approved local procedure.

SUMMARY
Every Scan. Every Time. Summary. Sensitive information must never become casual information. Security does not remove a person’s right to respectful treatment. Consistency prevents discrimination and builds trust. The response must never go further than the risk justifies.

STATE-01
Privacy. Protect the image and record through anonymity where required, authorised access, secure storage and lawful retention. Explain to the person how their information will be handled.

Key takeaway: Sensitive information must never become casual information. Why this matters: Sensitive information must never become casual information. Upper left: the protected display and lock illustrate privacy and authorised access.

STATE-02
Dignity. Treat the person as an individual: communicate respectfully, protect personal space and avoid unnecessary intrusion, embarrassment or coercion.

Key takeaway: Security does not remove a person’s right to respectful treatment. Why this matters: Security does not remove a person’s right to respectful treatment. Upper right: respectful explanation and personal space illustrate dignity.

STATE-03
Consistency. Apply the approved selection and scanning process fairly every time. Do not manipulate random selection or allow personal views to affect the process.

Key takeaway: Consistency prevents discrimination and builds trust. Why this matters: Consistency prevents discrimination and builds trust. Lower left: the group and selection symbol illustrate consistent neutral selection.

STATE-04
Proportionality. Use the least intrusive effective measure that achieves the lawful security purpose. If the next step is not necessary or not clearly authorised, stop and escalate.

Key takeaway: The response must never go further than the risk justifies. Why this matters: The response must never go further than the risk justifies. Lower right: the response alternatives illustrate proportionality. This conceptual picture does not specify a device’s radiation dose or authorise extra scans.
