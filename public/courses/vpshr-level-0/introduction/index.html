Welcome to VPSHR Level 0

Welcome to VPSHR Level Zero, the Voluntary Principles on Security and Human Rights. This course introduces the connection between responsible security and respect for people's rights. It gives us a shared foundation for the decisions we make at work.

Connect the learning to your workplace

You will explore human rights, the responsibilities of employees and security personnel, and situations that can put people at risk. As you learn, think about your own workplace. Who could be affected? What could go wrong? What would a responsible response look like?

One clear route through the course

Start with the learning unit shown on your course page. Within a presentation, use the slide arrows to move between slides. Use the platform's next unit button only when you are ready to leave the whole learning unit. This introduction is a single video, so it has no separate next button.

Learn at your own pace

You control the pace. Pause when you need time to think, replay an explanation, and turn on captions using the video controls. You can also enlarge the presentation or use full screen where those controls are available. You can return to an earlier unit to revisit its content.

Use activities and feedback to learn

Work through the activities and quizzes included in each learning unit. Read the instructions before answering, and check any stated pass mark or attempt limit. Use feedback to improve your understanding. Watching a video, or seeing a completion tick, does not by itself demonstrate competence.

Help is available when you need it

If you need help, use Messages and support on the platform. Tell us the course name, the learning unit, and the slide or activity where you got stuck. Describe what happened so that we can help you continue.

A valuable and rewarding learning journey

Take your time, ask questions, and connect what you learn to the way people are treated in practice. We wish you a valuable and rewarding learning journey. When this video finishes, stay on this page until you are ready. Then use the platform's next unit button to begin.
