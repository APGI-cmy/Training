SUMMARY
A Positive Indication: Lawful Response. Summary. Do not accuse, speculate or make a judgement. Reliable records protect the person and the investigation. The procedure tells you what is permitted. Bring in the correct authority promptly. Professional conduct is required at every stage.

STATE-01
Possible indication. A positive image is an indication that may require attention. It is not proof that the person has done anything wrong. Keep your language factual and your manner calm.

Key takeaway: Do not accuse, speculate or make a judgement. Why this matters: Do not accuse, speculate or make a judgement. Stage 1, left: the display and marked feature represent a possible indication requiring review.

Summon support. Notify the supervisor and, where required, police or other authorised support. The operator’s role is to report accurately and maintain the process, not to decide guilt.

STATE-05
Remain lawful. Treat the person humanely and respect their rights throughout. Detention is not punishment, and an indication is not a conviction.
