STATE-01
Legal basis and informed consent. A scan needs two foundations: a lawful authority in the applicable local law and an informed, voluntary agreement from the person. Explain the nature and purpose of the scan, the approved dose information and radiation-protection arrangements, and the record that will be kept. If the person is uncertain or cannot give informed consent, pause and refer the matter through the approved procedure.

Key takeaway: This prevents an operator from treating a scan as routine simply because the equipment is available. STATE-02
Privacy and record protection. A scan image and its record are sensitive information. Keep the viewer and subject appropriately anonymous where the process requires it, limit access to authorised personnel, store records securely and retain them only for the approved lawful period. Never share an image because someone is curious.

Key takeaway: Privacy protects the person and preserves the integrity of the operation. Why this matters: Privacy protects the person and preserves the integrity of the operation. Upper right: the protected display illustrates authorised access and secure records.

STATE-03
Dignity, consistency and neutral selection. Explain the process calmly, maintain personal space and never intimidate, ridicule or roughly treat a person. Apply the approved random-selection process exactly as it is generated. Neutral selection means you do not target or spare someone because of their appearance, background, status or relationship.

Key takeaway: Respect and consistency make the process humane and defensible. Why this matters: Respect and consistency make the process humane and defensible. Lower left: respectful communication and the neutral-selection symbol illustrate fair treatment.

STATE-04
Proportionality and escalation. Choose the least intrusive effective response that is justified by the security purpose. An indication or uncertainty is not permission to do more than necessary. Where the authority, consent, procedure or next step is unclear, do not guess; escalate to a supervisor.

Key takeaway: A lawful response protects security without unnecessarily limiting a person’s rights.
