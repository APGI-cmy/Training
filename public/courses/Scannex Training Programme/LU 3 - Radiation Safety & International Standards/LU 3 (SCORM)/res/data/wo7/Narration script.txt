SUMMARY
Quality Assurance and Records. Summary. Quality assurance combines routine testing, accurate records, active review and independent audit so faults and trends are found early.

STATE-01
Quality assurance. Under the supplied course protocol: The system is checked before first use and at least every three months. Image quality is checked using a suitable test phantom. Follow the current authorised site procedure and licence conditions.

Key takeaway: Follow the approved QA schedule and check that equipment is within its date. Why this matters: Follow the approved QA schedule and check that equipment is within its date. The Quarterly, QA and Image check labels illustrate the supplied course QA schedule and a suitable test phantom. Confirm the current site schedule with the RPO. A phantom provides a known reference so any decline can be detected objectively.

Key takeaway: Record every exposure and retain the required dose records. Why this matters: Record every exposure and retain the required dose records. Every exposure and 10-year retention are linked with protected records. This illustrates the supplied course protocol; confirm the current retention requirement. STATE-04
Review and escalate. Under the supplied course protocol: Dose records are checked weekly and monthly. A significant unexpected dose deviation must be reported immediately under the approved reporting process. Follow the current authorised site procedure and licence conditions.

Key takeaway: Review records on schedule and report significant unexpected deviations. Why this matters: Review records on schedule and report significant unexpected deviations. The Weekly and Monthly panels lead to checking and reporting a deviation. These intervals come from the supplied course protocol. Do not wait for the next review cycle if a serious variation is identified.
